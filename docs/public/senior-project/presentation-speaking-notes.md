# Presentation speaking notes

## Slide 1

State Mahita in one sentence: a trading terminal and strategy research platform that connects account context, strategy testing, and future live testing in one system.

## Slide 2

Explain the problem as workflow fragmentation. Do not start with features. Start with why the workflow is broken for smaller traders.

## Slide 3

Be honest about the two-quarter evolution. The project changed because the understanding of the real problem improved.

## Slide 4

Describe what works now:

- Mahita accounts
- Schwab developer-key onboarding
- Schwab brokerage connection
- dashboard, portfolio, trader, and backtester prototype

## Slide 5

Use architecture language:

- identity
- session and role checks
- brokerage authorization
- operator/control plane
- product surfaces

## Slide 6

Emphasize trust boundaries. This is one of the strongest engineering parts of the project.

## Slide 7

Move through the demo quickly and confidently. Show flow, not every control.

## Slide 8

Say what is unfinished without apologizing. Phrase it as engineering constraints and next steps.

## Slide 9

Frame ML as the next disciplined research track, not a buzzword feature.

## Slide 10

End with what you learned about system boundaries, APIs, security, and platform thinking.

## What to avoid saying

- do not claim the platform is production-ready
- do not imply ML strategy optimization is already implemented
- do not claim the current backtester is commercial-grade
- do not oversell live automation
